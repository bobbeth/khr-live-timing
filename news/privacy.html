Privacy & analytics choices

Privacy notice · 5 November 2026. We use aggregate analytics to understand which parts of Mud Mules TV people visit. Collection is enabled by default, subject to the choices below.

Your choices

You can disable analytics or exclude your own browser without losing access to any part of the website. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track also disable this counter. Your choice is saved as a browser preference, not a visitor identity. An earlier analytics decline remains respected.

What the counter records

The counter records approved website sections and public content identifiers: race articles, driver profiles and broadcast pages. Driver customer IDs identify the driver page being viewed, not the person visiting it. It also records a small arrival-source category such as Facebook, Discord, YouTube, Twitch, search engines, other websites or Direct / Unknown. Approved promotional source labels can supply that category. Missing referral information stays unknown; a label is not proof of the actual source.

We use our own small counter and Cloudflare-hosted collection/storage, independently of Google Analytics. We do not use advertising trackers, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, persistent visitor cookies or cross-site identity matching. We do not send names, email addresses, search text, form contents, chat messages, arbitrary URL queries or full referral addresses. SDK telemetry and race-data API requests are not visitor events.

Page views and estimated sessions

Each accepted navigation contributes a recorded page view. A random identifier exists only in the webpage's memory for up to 30 minutes to estimate sessions. It is never saved in browser cookies or storage. Reloading the page or opening another tab starts another estimate. This is not an exact count of unique people. No individual browsing history is stored in the reporting database.

Temporary one-way hashes of random event and tab identifiers are used for duplicate detection. Their useful lifetimes are one hour and 30 minutes respectively; automated hourly cleanup removes expired records. Aggregates are kept for 400 days, alongside lifetime totals. D1's automatic backup history can retain older snapshots for seven days on its free plan; that includes snapshots of temporary token hashes. This seven-day recovery retention forms part of our approved analytics setup.

Network processing and protection

Cloudflare and GitHub necessarily receive connection information when delivering or accepting web requests. Our analytics database does not store raw IP addresses, full user-agent strings or full referring URLs. The collector uses a short-window keyed hash of connection information only for abuse limiting, separately from visitor-session estimation. Request/payload logging is disabled in the Worker configuration. Hosting and security-provider processing remains subject to their policies and the approved deployment settings.

The owner dashboard requires authentication and shows aggregates only. Recorded counts can miss blocked or offline requests and can include undetected automation. They do not prove video plays, watch time or exact audience size.

External services

YouTube recordings and thumbnails, SDK Gaming driver photographs and external links are separate services. They can make their own requests when you use those features; the analytics choices here control the MMTV counter only.
