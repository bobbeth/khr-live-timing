Privacy & analytics choices

Updated 2 November 2026.

Your analytics choice

Analytics preferences require JavaScript. Without JavaScript, the analytics tag is not loaded.

What we measure

When you allow analytics, Mud Mules TV uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits, sections viewed and clicks on external links, including YouTube. Google can report approximate geography, browser/device information, referral sources and engagement. Analytics cookies distinguish browsers; visitor counts are estimates, not a list of identified people.

Tracking is off until you choose Allow. Declining does not restrict the website. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals also disable this analytics integration. Advertising storage, advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled in the site's tag configuration.

We send section labels, race/video IDs and verified public driver IDs as content information. We do not send your search-box text, form contents, arbitrary URL queries, full referring-page addresses, email addresses or a visitor account ID through our tracking code. External link events include the destination domain rather than its full address. A YouTube link click is not proof that someone watched a video.

Storage and preferences

Your choice is remembered in this browser for up to 180 days. If you allow analytics, the site's analytics cookies use an expiry of 90 days, refreshed by activity. You can decline later or exclude your own browser above. Exclusion lasts until you reverse it or clear browser storage. Withdrawing consent stops this site's analytics and removes its prefixed analytics cookies; it does not erase data already collected by Google.

Google and external media

Google processes analytics data under its own policies. See Google's privacy policy and Google Analytics privacy information.

YouTube players and thumbnails, SDK Gaming driver photographs and other external links are separate services. Their requests and storage are not controlled by the analytics buttons on this page. Viewing a recording can connect your browser to YouTube. Driver biographies and social links are supplied by drivers and are separate from visitor analytics.
